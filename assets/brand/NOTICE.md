# Turbine logo

`turbine-logo.svg` is the Turbine logo as published on Turbine's website. Turbine and its logo belong to PropellerHeads. turbine-cli is an unofficial community tool: it draws the logo in the terminal (as Braille frames generated by `npm run logo:build`) only to show which service it works with, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Turbine or PropellerHeads.
